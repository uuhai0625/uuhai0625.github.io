About uuhai

My day job is at a regular company. Everything on this site is a side project I build alone, in the gaps between work and on weekends. I can't draw, and I've never made a video. What got me started was simply how interesting it felt that generative AI had come so far in just a few years that one person could create a character, turn it into stickers, put it in a picture book, and build tools and videos around it too.

One thing I care about is not showing only the parts that worked. Honestly, most of my prompts don't come out the way I want, and I've scrapped plenty of characters and ideas partway through. I put all of that out here, the trial and error and the bad calls included, in the hope that it's useful as an "experiment log" for anyone curious about side projects or making things on their own.

Right now I'm running eight lines in parallel: LINE stickers, note articles, picture books, practical calculators, Chrome extensions, one-time-purchase tools, video, and an investing blog. Each one is an experiment in how far AI can take me. Some stall out without much to show for it, and some slowly take shape the longer I keep at them. This page is where I keep track of the work in progress.

Why work on so many different things at once?
Because the point isn't to pick one thing I'm good at. It's to see how far one person can stretch with generative AI. Each genre has its own process and its own difficulties, so I keep going with all of them and compare as I go.
Is everything made with AI?
I handle the planning, the concepts, and the final quality checks myself. I use generative AI heavily for illustration, first drafts of text, video generation, and so on, and I say so clearly on each piece of content.
How often do you update?
It depends on the line. note gets a post every day, picture books aim for about one a week, and the rest are updated whenever production gets there.

LINE Stickers

Can you build an AI character, turn it into stickers, and actually sell them? This is the series where I'm still finding out, solo. It started with Kutao, a salaryman with no filter, and grew from there: Tasogare, a tattered ghost; Zubizuba, a mysterious figure dressed all in black; Murineko, a cat who turns down every invitation; and Azatoi, a seal who teases everyone. Each character deliberately goes in a completely different direction. Sales matter less to me than the real question: can I build a character from scratch with AI and make it hold up as a brand?

Picture Books (Amazon KDP)

A series of children's picture books illustrated with generative AI, with a new volume roughly every week. I started it as a challenge to do the whole thing myself, from generating the illustrations to writing the text to self-publishing on Kindle, with nothing outsourced. Practical Calculators

A set of completely free web calculators for money questions tied to Japanese life events. Things like how much to give at a wedding or funeral, or how to split expenses for a side business, tend to get vague answers when you search for them. I wanted a place where you could get a concrete number right away. No sign-up, no payment. Just open it when you need it, check the figure, and close it.

Sōba Note A free guide to the customary cash-gift amounts for weddings, funerals, new babies, and other life events in Japan, where giving a set amount of money in an envelope is the norm. Visit site AI Side Hustle Abacus Calculators and a gear guide for people running a side business with generative AI.
